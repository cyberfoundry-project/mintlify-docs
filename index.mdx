# Welcome

Welcome to the CyberFoundry Project Community Docs. This site serves as a central hub for our documentation, platform guides, and a consistent source of information across the project.

CyberFoundry is an actively evolving, community-driven platform focused on advancing the Information Technology and Cybersecurity ecosystem. It enables professionals to design, share, and refine the tools, playbooks, and practices they rely on every day, transforming individual expertise into collective, actionable resources.

## Our Mission

Our mission is to empower the global IT and Cybersecurity community by turning shared expertise into practical tools and playbooks, collectively building the capabilities needed to secure and advance the industry.

## Community-Driven

CyberFoundry is built for IT and Cybersecurity professionals to connect, collaborate, and share knowledge. By bringing together industry expertise in one place, the platform helps solve real-world technical challenges and supports the continuous development of resources the community depends on.
